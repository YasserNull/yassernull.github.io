LogCat Live Wallpaper - Privacy Policy

LogCat Live Wallpaper Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Overview

LogCat Live Wallpaper is an open-source Android application that stops background applications using Root or Shizuku permissions.

Data Collection

LogCat Live Wallpaper does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

Permissions

Root and Shizuku permissions are used only to manage and stop background applications.

Data Sharing

LogCat Live Wallpaper does not share user data with third parties.

Children's Privacy

LogCat Live Wallpaper does not collect data from children or any other users.

Policy Changes

Any updates to this privacy policy will be published on this page.

Contact

For questions, visit our GitHub profile.
